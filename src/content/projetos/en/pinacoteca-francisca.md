## Context

Digital installation for the 50th anniversary exhibition of the UFV Art Gallery, shown from November 7 to 18, 2023. Tarsila do Amaral's portrait “Francisca” was made to speak.

## What I did

- Used MediaPipe to track facial movement in a reference video.
- Manually adjusted facial landmarks to align them with the illustration.
- Applied video warping to deform the portrait naturally.
