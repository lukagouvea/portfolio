## Data

The dataset is PlantVillage, in the version with the background removed: 54,306 leaf photos in 38 classes, each one a species paired with a disease, or the healthy leaf. There are 14 species; tomato alone has 10 classes.

The classes are imbalanced, ranging from 152 images (healthy potato) to 5,507 (orange with greening). I split 80% for training and 20% for validation, and sampled training images with weights inversely proportional to class frequency.

## What I did

- Transfer learning by fine-tuning pretrained CNN backbones in PyTorch to classify multiple leaf diseases.
- Preprocessing and data augmentation pipelines with NumPy and PyTorch DataLoaders.
- Adapted the classification head and tuned hyperparameters to improve generalization and control overfitting.

## Result

95% accuracy on the validation set.
