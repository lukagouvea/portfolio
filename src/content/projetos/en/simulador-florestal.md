## Context

Nutree is a research center at UFV focused on forest nutrition and soils. Its researchers use a desktop application to simulate forest production. The legacy system had limitations that hurt the reliability of their scientific workflows.

## What I did

- Rebuilt the platform, and I still maintain it.
- Parallel processing with threading in the large-scale computation pipelines. Together with optimized SQL queries, this gave a 95% performance gain in the simulation engine.
- Data layer with SQLAlchemy: the ORM for modeling complex entities and Core for analytical queries.
- Distribution pipeline for the software, with automatic updates, release management and versioning.
- Monitoring with Sentry, user documentation and direct technical support for researchers.
- Coordinated a small team, including mentoring an intern, and aligned requirements with the program's researchers and managers.
