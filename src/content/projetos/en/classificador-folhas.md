## What I did

- Transfer learning by fine-tuning pretrained CNN backbones in PyTorch to classify multiple leaf diseases.
- Preprocessing and data augmentation pipelines with NumPy and PyTorch DataLoaders.
- Adapted the classification head and tuned hyperparameters to improve generalization and control overfitting.

## Result

95% accuracy on the validation set.
